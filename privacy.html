Kutout – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Kutout is published by Kart Production.

What we collect

Nothing. Images you choose are processed on your own device and are never uploaded to us or any server. We have no accounts, analytics, ads or trackers.

Network use

The first time you remove a background, the app may download the open-source AI model files (from the app itself, or from IMG.LY's public file host). Those requests do not include your images.

If you use the poster text tool online, fonts may be loaded from Google Fonts, which can see your device's IP address like any website that loads them. Your images are not sent.

Permissions

The app only reads images you pick yourself and saves or shares the images you export.

Children

Kutout does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
